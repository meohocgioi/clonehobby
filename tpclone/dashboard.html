Preview a post (nothing is sent)

Repost older posts by date

For HPOI the app scrolls down the website's Latest Info list page by page (like you do) until it is past that day. Cards only say “3 days ago”, so an item at the edge of a day can land on the neighbouring date. A far-back date takes a while (one page every 5 seconds).

Queued reposts are published by the worker (press Start) at the configured delay; they never repeat posts already published.

Settings

First, connect your Telegram channel (one time, 2 minutes):
  1. In Telegram open @BotFather → send /newbot → follow the steps. It gives you a long token; paste it below.
  2. Open your channel → Administrators → Add Administrator → search your new bot → allow Post messages.
  3. Type your channel name below (like @mychannel). For a private channel use its number (starts with -100).

Backup

Moved or re-downloaded the app and it forgot what it already posted? Choose your old tpclone.db (or a file from the old backups folder, or a downloaded backup). Posts it knew as posted are added to Toy-People, so they won't be posted twice. Nothing is deleted. (Download backups from the dashboard panels.)

Advanced: updates

Updating (button at the top right of the dashboard) downloads the newest version, tests it, restarts the app by itself (a post in progress finishes first) and reloads the page. Your settings and posted-lists are never touched. If the new version fails to start, the old one comes back automatically.
