Repost older posts by date

For HPOI the app scrolls down the website's Latest Info list page by page (like you do) until it is past that day. Cards only say “3 days ago”, so an item at the edge of a day can land on the neighbouring date. A far-back date takes a while (one page every 5 seconds).

Queued reposts are published by the worker (press Start) at the configured delay; they never repeat posts already published.

  1. In Telegram open @BotFather → send /newbot → follow the steps. It gives you a long token; paste it below.
  2. Open your channel → Administrators → Add Administrator → choose your new bot → allow Post messages.
  3. Type your channel name below (like @mychannel) and press Save changes.

Backups

Every day the app saves a copy of each posted-list and keeps the last 14 days, so a post is never published twice — even after moving the app.

Data folder

Restore

Import an old posted-list
Moved or re-downloaded the app and it forgot what it posted? Choose your old tpclone.db or a backup file. Posts it knew are added to Toy-People; nothing is deleted.

Updates

The app can update itself: it downloads the newest version, tests it and restarts. Your settings and posted-lists are never touched; if the new version fails to start, the old one comes back.
